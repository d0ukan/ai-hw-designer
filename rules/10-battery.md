## 10 · Battery and charging — conditional

> **Apply only if the board has a rechargeable battery or runs on a battery** (INT-52). Otherwise this section is N/A and no battery questions are asked. If a certified off-the-shelf battery pack or module is used, the manufacturer's guide is binding.

- [ ] **BAT-01** — Obtain from the user: chemistry (Li-ion, LiPo, LiFePO4, NiMH, primary/non-rechargeable), cell count (S/P), capacity, nominal and maximum voltage, peak charge/discharge current, operating and charging temperature, whether the cell has a protection circuit, and required certifications (e.g. IEC 62133, UN 38.3; in Aviation e.g. RTCA DO-311A). Do not guess unknown values.
- [ ] **BAT-02** — **Protection:** Lithium cells must have overcharge, overdischarge, overcurrent and short-circuit protection (protection IC + dual MOSFET). If the cell lacks it, design it on the board. Use reverse polarity protection and a fuse or PTC on the main battery line.
- [ ] **BAT-03** — **Charging:** Choose a charger IC suited to the chemistry (Li-ion CC/CV 4.20 V or the value in the cell datasheet; LiFePO4 3.60 V). Set the charge current from the cell datasheet and the input source limit (e.g. USB 2.0 500 mA; on USB-C, CC resistors and source current advertisement). If charging is needed while the load runs, use power-path management.
- [ ] **BAT-04** — **Temperature:** Gate charging to a temperature window with an NTC (typically 0…45 °C for charging; apply JEITA if supported). The NTC must be in thermal contact with the cell; the charger IC's thermal pad and vias must follow the datasheet.
- [ ] **BAT-05** — **Layout:** Keep the battery current path short and wide (LAY-20); Kelvin-connect current-sense resistors. The battery connector must be polarized, non-reversible and locking if needed; marked `+` / `−` on the silkscreen. Keep the cell and connector away from heat sources.
- [ ] **BAT-06** — **Measurement and sleep current:** Make the battery voltage divider high-value or switched so it does not draw continuous current. Calculate and record the sleep/standby current budget. If remaining capacity is needed, evaluate a fuel gauge.
- [ ] **BAT-07** — A primary (non-rechargeable) cell is never connected to a charging circuit. If multiple power sources are in parallel (e.g. USB + battery), block reverse current with an ideal diode, Schottky or power-path.
- [ ] **BAT-08** — **Verification:** Add charge profile, protection thresholds, temperature cutoff, short-circuit behavior (in a safe test setup) and sleep current measurement to the test plan. Record an untested item as "not verified"; the Reviewer checks the evidence.
